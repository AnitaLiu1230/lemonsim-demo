Frequently Asked Questions

View all
Does my phone support eSIM?

Your phone needs eSIM support and must be carrier-unlocked. Quick check: dial *#06#. If a 32-digit EID appears, the phone has eSIM hardware. You can also check your phone model.

When should I install my eSIM?

Install it on Wi-Fi shortly before you need mobile data. Your plan starts as soon as you install it, and the day you install it counts as day 1. Each new plan day begins at noon EDT (11:00 a.m. EST), wherever you are. Turning the eSIM off doesn't pause it. Don't delete it afterwards: a QR code can usually be used only once.

Can I use hotspot?

Yes. All LemonSIM plans allow hotspot (tethering), so you can share your data with a laptop or another device.

Can I top up my plan?

Physical SIM cards can be topped up at topup.lemonsim.com. eSIM plans can't be topped up: if you need more data or days, buy a new eSIM plan.

Before you buy, make sure your phone is compatible and unlocked.
