Check in Settings

The quickest way to be sure is to look on the phone itself.

  • iPhone: Settings › General › About. An "EID" or "Digital SIM" entry means eSIM is supported.
  • Android: Settings › Connections › SIM manager. An "Add eSIM" option means eSIM is supported (names vary by brand).

What does carrier-unlocked mean?

A locked phone only works with the carrier that sold it. On iPhone, Settings › General › About › Carrier Lock should say "No SIM restrictions". On Android, ask your carrier or try another carrier's SIM.

Using a LemonSIM physical SIM card

A LemonSIM card works in a phone or device that:

  • is carrier-unlocked
  • supports 4G LTE (5G phones work too)
  • has a free SIM slot (cards are available in nano, micro and standard sizes)

On dual-SIM phones you can usually keep your home SIM in the other slot. Some phones share a slot between a second SIM and a memory card.

Your phone should also support the 4G bands used at your destination. You'll find them in your phone's specifications.

Common questions

My phone isn't on the list. Can I still use an eSIM?

Possibly. Our list isn't complete. Dial *#06# or look in Settings: if you see an EID or an "Add eSIM" option, your phone has eSIM.

Why does it matter where I bought my phone?

Some models are sold without eSIM in certain countries. The note under each brand in the list tells you which versions to watch for.

How do I know if my phone is carrier-unlocked?

Phones bought outright from the manufacturer or a retailer are usually unlocked. If yours came with a carrier contract, ask your carrier. On iPhone, Settings › General › About › Carrier Lock should say "No SIM restrictions".

What if my phone doesn't support eSIM?

A LemonSIM physical SIM card works in any unlocked phone with a free SIM slot. It's delivered to your address, so order it a week or so before you travel.
