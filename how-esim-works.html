How eSIM Works

Set up your eSIM in two parts: before you leave, and when you land.

Last reviewed by the LemonSIM Support team · November 3, 2026 · Editorial standards

Get ready

  1. 1

    Check your phone

    It needs eSIM support and must be carrier-unlocked. .

  2. 2

    Know when your plan starts

    Your plan starts when you install the eSIM, and the day you install it counts as day 1. Each new plan day begins at noon Eastern Daylight Time (EDT), or 11:00 a.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST), regardless of your location. For example, a 7-day plan installed at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time on May 1 expires at noon Eastern Time on May 7.

  3. 3

    Install it on Wi-Fi

    Shortly before you need mobile data, scan the QR code from your email.

    • iPhone: Settings › Cellular (or Mobile Service) › Add eSIM
    • Android: Settings › Connections › SIM manager › Add eSIM (names vary by brand)
    • Don't delete the eSIM later: a QR code can usually be used only once.
  4. 4

    Label the line

    Name it, for example "LemonSIM Japan", so it's easy to pick for mobile data. Turning it off doesn't pause the plan.

When you land

  1. 5

    Turn it on with data roaming

    Switch the LemonSIM line on and turn on data roaming for that line.

  2. 6

    Use it for mobile data

    Choose LemonSIM for mobile data. Your home SIM can stay on for calls and texts.

  3. 7

    Turn off roaming on your home line

    Turn off data roaming on your home SIM so your home carrier doesn't charge you.

No connection after a few minutes? See Support.
